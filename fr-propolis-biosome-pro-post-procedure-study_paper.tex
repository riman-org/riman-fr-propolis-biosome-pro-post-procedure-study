\begin{abstract}
Background: The French aesthetic market demands high-efficacy yet gentle formulations for post-procedure recovery, particularly for fragile barriers compromised by laser or microneedling. This study evaluates the patented Propolis Biosome-Pro complex from RIMAN Skincare, focusing on its microfluidizer-enabled penetration and soothing properties. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 consumers and B2B practitioners across Lyon, Paris, and Marseille assessed texture, scent neutrality, clinical trust, and regulatory compliance using Likert scales and NPS metrics. Results: The formulation achieved a composite mean of 3.59 with a Top-2-Box score of 57.3\%. Notably, texture lightness scored highest (Mean 4.14, T2B 99.0\%), indicating exceptional suitability for occlusion-prone sensitive skin. Immediate soothing relief was highly rated (Mean 4.0, T2B 91.5\%). However, olfactory neutrality (Mean 3.48) and INCI clarity regarding safety profiles (Mean 2.93) emerged as critical friction points. Strategic Implications: While clinical validation is the primary driver for adoption (97\% selection rate), lack of long-term data (95.5\%) remains the top barrier. RIMAN must prioritize transparent communication of the 'Encapsulated Propolis Extract' benefits and leverage technical support hotlines to overcome B2B hesitation.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} RIMAN Skincare, Propolis Biosome-Pro, Post-Procedure Recovery, Microclimate Adaptation, French Dermatology, Barrier Maintenance
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Formulation Architecture \& Study Methodology}
The study focuses on the RIMAN R\&D Ontology product 'Propolis Biosome-Pro,' a patented complex designed for Skin Hydration, Barrier Maintenance, and Antioxidant/Soothing effects. Utilizing microfluidizer technology, the formulation aims to stabilize encapsulated propolis extract for deeper penetration without irritation. The methodology involved a stratified sample of 200 participants in France, segmented by city microclimates (Lyon, Paris, Marseille), skin types, and professional status (C-End vs. B2B). Key metrics included Likert-scale responses on sensory attributes, clinical trust, and regulatory confidence, alongside Net Promoter Score (NPS) analysis to gauge recommendation likelihood within the French aesthetic dermatology community.
\section{2. Quantitative Performance \& Microclimate Adaptation}
Quantitative results reveal strong performance in physical application attributes. The texture's lightweight nature received the highest rating (Mean 4.14, SD 0.38, T2B 99.0\%), confirming its non-occlusive profile suitable for post-procedure skin in varying humidity levels (39\%-56\% RH). Immediate soothing efficacy also performed well (Mean 4.0, T2B 91.5\%). Conversely, sensory acceptance of the encapsulated propolis scent was moderate (Mean 3.48, T2B 49.0\%), suggesting that while tolerated, it may not be perceived as fully neutral by patients with heightened olfactory sensitivity. Regional analysis showed slight variations, with Lyon (Continental) scoring marginally higher (Mean 3.63) than Paris and Marseille (both Mean 3.57), potentially due to differing winter dryness impacts on barrier perception.
\section{3. Cross-Demographic, MBTI \& B2B/C-End Stratification}
Demographic stratification highlights distinct drivers. Affluent/High Net Worth individuals reported the highest satisfaction (Mean 3.81), followed by Upper-Middle Income (3.64). In terms of occupation, B2B Clinic Owners demonstrated the highest endorsement (Mean 3.80), valuing clinical outcomes over cost. MBTI analysis reveals that pragmatic types like ENTJ (Mean 3.70) and ESTJ (Mean 3.63) align closely with the product's efficiency claims, whereas INTPs (Mean 3.42) expressed skepticism regarding mechanism transparency. For B2B integration, 'Technical Support Hotline' (100\% selection) and 'Staff Training Modules' (95\%) are deemed critical, far outweighing co-marketing budgets (12.5\%). Adoption drivers are heavily skewed toward evidence-based factors: 'Clinical Validation Data' (97\%) and 'Proven Barrier Repair' (96\%).
\section{4. Regulatory Compliance \& Regional Expansion Strategy}
Regulatory confidence is moderately high but requires reinforcement. Trust in EU cosmetic regulation compliance averaged 3.66 (T2B 65.5\%), yet understanding of the specific INCI declaration 'Encapsulated Propolis Extract' was low (Mean 2.93, T2B 4.0\%), indicating a communication gap between scientific innovation and consumer/regulator literacy. The primary competitor benchmark remains La Roche-Posay Cicaplast (58.5\% selection), positioning Propolis Biosome-Pro as a premium alternative. To expand, RIMAN must address the top barriers: 'Lack of Long-Term Data' (95.5\%) and 'High Cost per Treatment' (90.0\%). Strategies should include publishing longitudinal clinical trials and offering robust B2B trial kits to mitigate risk perception among clinic owners.

\section{Conclusion}
The Propolis Biosome-Pro complex demonstrates superior textural compatibility and immediate soothing benefits for post-procedure fragile skin in the French market, achieving a 99.0\% Top-2-Box score for lightweight texture. However, significant opportunities exist in enhancing olfactory neutrality and clarifying the safety narrative of the encapsulated propolis INCI. B2B adoption is contingent upon rigorous clinical validation and comprehensive technical support rather than marketing incentives. By addressing the deficit in long-term data visibility and optimizing price-value communication, RIMAN can effectively displace incumbent dermo-cosmetic benchmarks like La Roche-Posay, establishing Propolis Biosome-Pro as the gold standard for advanced barrier stabilization protocols.
