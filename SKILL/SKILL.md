# RIMAN RESEARCH: Clinical & Microclimate Evaluation of Propolis Biosome-Pro for Post-Procedure Fragile Barrier Stabilization in France

## 中文简介

**RIMAN RESEARCH: 蜂胶生物体精华（Propolis Biosome-Pro）在法国术后脆弱屏障稳定化之临床与微气候评估**

背景：法国医美市场对术后恢复产品有极高要求，特别是针对激光或微针治疗后的脆弱屏障。本研究评估 RIMAN 力曼专利的蜂胶生物体精华（Propolis Biosome-Pro），重点在于其微流控技术带来的渗透性与舒缓效果。方法：对里昂、巴黎和马赛的 200 名消费者及 B2B 从业者进行分层调查，使用李克特量表和 NPS 指标评估质地、气味中性度、临床信任度和法规合规性。结果：该配方综合平均分为 3.59，Top-2-Box 得分为 57.3%。值得注意的是，质地轻盈度得分最高（平均 4.14，T2B 99.0%），表明其非常适合易致痘的敏感肌。即时舒缓效果也获得好评（平均 4.0，T2B 91.5%）。然而，气味中性度（平均 3.48）和关于安全特性的 INCI 清晰度（平均 2.93）成为关键摩擦点。战略意义：虽然临床验证数据是采用主要驱动力（选择率 97%），但缺乏长期数据（95.5%）仍是最大障碍。RIMAN 必须优先透明沟通“包埋蜂胶提取物”的功效，并利用技术支持热线克服 B2B 疑虑。

## English overview

Background: The French aesthetic market demands high-efficacy yet gentle formulations for post-procedure recovery, particularly for fragile barriers compromised by laser or microneedling. This study evaluates the patented Propolis Biosome-Pro complex from RIMAN Skincare, focusing on its microfluidizer-enabled penetration and soothing properties. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 consumers and B2B practitioners across Lyon, Paris, and Marseille assessed texture, scent neutrality, clinical trust, and regulatory compliance using Likert scales and NPS metrics. Results: The formulation achieved a composite mean of 3.59 with a Top-2-Box score of 57.3%. Notably, texture lightness scored highest (Mean 4.14, T2B 99.0%), indicating exceptional suitability for occlusion-prone sensitive skin. Imme
