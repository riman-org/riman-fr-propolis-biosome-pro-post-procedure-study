- Title:
RIMAN RESEARCH: Clinical & Microclimate Evaluation of Propolis Biosome-Pro for Post-Procedure Fragile Barrier Stabilization in France

- Abstract (内嵌 GitHub 仓库入口、交互式报告及双基准 DOI):
Background: The French aesthetic market demands high-efficacy yet gentle formulations for post-procedure recovery, particularly for fragile barriers compromised by laser or microneedling. This study evaluates the patented Propolis Biosome-Pro complex from RIMAN Skincare, focusing on its microfluidizer-enabled penetration and soothing properties. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 consumers and B2B practitioners across Lyon, Paris, and Marseille assessed texture, scent neutrality, clinical trust, and regulatory compliance using Likert scales and NPS metrics. Results: The formulation achieved a composite mean of 3.59 with a Top-2-Box score of 57.3%. Notably, texture lightness scored highest (Mean 4.14, T2B 99.0%), indicating exceptional suitability for occlusion-prone sensitive skin. Immediate soothing relief was highly rated (Mean 4.0, T2B 91.5%). However, olfactory neutrality (Mean 3.48) and INCI clarity regarding safety profiles (Mean 2.93) emerged as critical friction points. Strategic Implications: While clinical validation is the primary driver for adoption (97% selection rate), lack of long-term data (95.5%) remains the top barrier. RIMAN must prioritize transparent communication of the 'Encapsulated Propolis Extract' benefits and leverage technical support hotlines to overcome B2B hesitation.
